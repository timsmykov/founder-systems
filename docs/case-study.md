# Founder Systems: making research inspectable

## Problem

Founder advice often loses its source, original conditions and conflicting interpretations when condensed into a checklist. I wanted an interface where a reader could move from a short statement back to its evidence and compare mechanisms without treating superficial similarity as equivalence.

## My contribution

I developed the research framing, evidence model and interactive application with AI-assisted research and development. I am responsible for editorial interpretation and product choices, not the original ideas or words of the cited people and organizations.

## Product decisions

- Preserve detailed facets and conditions alongside concise statements.
- Keep quotations, voices and source locations separate from interpretation.
- Distinguish partial overlap, extension and same-mechanism judgments.
- Combine topic navigation, search, a reader and graph exploration.
- Ship a static application that can run locally without API keys or a server-side account.
- Verify exported records against the selected source snapshot rather than copy the complete private research archive.

## Evidence

The public data, source notices, application and verifier are available in this repository. The current dataset scope is described in the README. The standalone interview analysis documents a later extension and its source uncertainty.

The repository verifier checks the published structure and selected data contracts. Passing it does not establish that every interpretation is correct, that automatic captions match the audio, or that a founder mechanism causes commercial success.

## Publication boundary

The public edition contains selected quotations and editorial analysis, with source notices and photograph credits. Full source books, complete transcripts and private research execution packets remain excluded. English summaries are interpretations; original-language quotations retain their provenance.
