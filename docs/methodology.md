# Research scope

The current map preserves 488 Nik / QuantumLight meanings with 2,273 facets and 295 book-derived meanings with 1,419 facets. Consolidated cards retain their normalized member variants and detailed evidence references. The 249 comparison groups preserve their reviewed actions, conditions, differences, counterevidence and attribution.

Relationship types distinguish the same mechanism, partial overlap, the same goal with different methods, complementary methods and insufficient evidence. The 23 same-mechanism groups are not proof of 23 independent universal principles. Reading categories organise navigation; they do not establish agreement.

Literal quotations are evidence from the collected sources, not newly authenticated recordings. Speaker attribution distinguishes Nik, institutional QuantumLight, Musk via the curated book, editors, interviewers and other voices. Supporting context is retained separately. Source hashes establish consistency with the collected snapshot, not source truth or legal clearance.

The private research archive retains full source files and earlier reversible analysis passes. The public library preserves the original normalized card wording, literal evidence and reviewed comparison decisions while omitting raw sources and execution packets. Public file hashes are listed in `release-manifest.json`.
