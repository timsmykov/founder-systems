## Changes

Both display portraits use AI-assisted background removal and a tight head-and-shoulders crop, followed by WebP encoding. GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, xhigh quality, was explicitly selected. The transparent result is placed over CSS surfaces that follow the active theme. These are adaptations of identified real photographs, not newly invented photographic sources. Original files, credits, licences and hashes remain available. No endorsement by either person, photographer or source organisation is implied.
